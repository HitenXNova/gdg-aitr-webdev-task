# GDG AITR Website Audit & Suggestions

I spent some time going through the website on both my phone and laptop to check how everything looks and works. Here’s what I noticed and a few ideas that could make the portal even better.

---

## 1. Things That Need a Fix

### Mobile Layout Issues
* **Horizontal Scroll:** On smaller phone screens, the page scrolls sideways a bit because some elements spill past the edge.
* **Menu Overlay:** The drop-down menu on mobile covers up the logo at the top when you open it.
* **Text Overflow on Cards:** In the "What happens next?" section on phone screens, long text like email addresses spills past the right edge of the card, cutting off words at the border. Screenshot of the issue is uploaded here.

### Design & Performance
* **Awkward Text Wrapping:** On phone screens, main titles break into 3 or 4 weirdly short lines.

---

## 2. Feature Ideas for the Portal

If we want to make the website more interactive for students, here are a few sections that would be awesome to add:

1. **Event Pass & Countdown:** A section on the homepage with a countdown timer for upcoming events, where students can register and get a digital pass badge.
2. **Student Project Showcase:** A place where members can share their open-source projects (Web, AI, Mobile) and let others view their GitHub links.
3. **Resource Hub:** A collection of learning roadmaps, workshop slides, and useful developer tools categorized by topic so beginners know where to start.
4. **Active Contributor Wall:** A simple leaderboard or highlight section to feature students who actively contribute to GDG events and repos.
